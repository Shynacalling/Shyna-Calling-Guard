# Shyna Caller Guard Premium v2.2

## Live Google / Samsung contact sync
- Reads contacts directly from Android Contacts Provider.
- Google Contacts and Samsung Contacts changes appear automatically after the phone syncs them.
- Uses ContentObserver to refresh contact suggestions and contact lists while the app is open.
- Displays saved contact photos where available.
- Shows a source badge for Google, Samsung, SIM, or Phone contacts.
- Does not create a separate private contact database.

The phone's own account sync settings control when Google or Samsung downloads/uploads contact changes.
