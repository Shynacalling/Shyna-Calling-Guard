# v4.9.5 Video Layout

- Remote participant video fills the complete video call screen.
- Local camera preview is a small rounded movable window.
- Dragging the local preview is constrained to safe screen bounds.
- Tap the video area to show or hide controls.
- Controls use a standard three-column layout with subtle press and slide/fade animations.
- Speaker, camera, mute, camera switch, remote visibility, and end-call actions remain connected.
